# GEO / AI search strategy

## Priority: entity clarity, not AI hacks
The name **Dam Ngoc Tu** can overlap with unrelated public figures. Keep the website connected to the correct employer and investor-relations remit and do **not** attach profiles or biographical facts based on name-only matches. The canonical Person `@id` is shared by all site pages; Organization has a real public website. The exact role title requires independent organisational confirmation.

## Pages and likely AI questions
| Natural-language query | Landing page | Evidence gap |
|---|---|---|
| Who is Dam Ngoc Tu at IAG Global? | `/about.html` | Employer-confirmed title, official linked profile and real biography |
| What does Dam Ngoc Tu oversee in investor relations? | `/` and `/expertise.html` | Documented scope from IAG leadership; user brief only |
| How can I contact Dam Ngoc Tu for investor relations? | `/contact.html` | Verified delivery, official contact channel |
| What is investor readiness for a cross-border discussion? | `/expertise.html` | Reviewed supporting references / documented practical framework |
| How does investor relations improve information governance? | `/insights.html` | Signed original insight or sourced examples |
| What investor opportunities does IAG offer? | No dedicated landing page | Do not imply an offer without verified disclosures |

## Content quality
- Existing plain-HTML copy, titles and headings are crawlable without executing scripts.
- Keep concrete investor-relations definitions accessible in the HTML; client-side interactive variants are enhancements, not the only source of essential content.
- Six editorial pieces share one URL; for stronger topical retrieval, publish separately only when genuine editorial review, stable citations and meaningful unique content are available.
- No fake awards, credentials, investments or third-party testimonials. Verify actual career milestones, published interviews and approved corporate media before adding.
- There is no special Google AIO/GEO schema, and `llms.txt` is not a ranking factor. Official guidance: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide

## Measure
After going live, use URL Inspection, page-level Search Console data, Google generative AI reports where accessible, and targeted manual query checks. Track organic search and AI referrals when observable. Never infer AI citations from a sitemap.
