How enquiries are handled.

A concise notice explaining the intended handling of information submitted through the website.

Enquiry information

If the contact form is configured, it collects the name, email, organisation, region, enquiry type and message voluntarily provided. This information is used to review and respond to the enquiry, not for public posting or automated investment profiling.

Processing and retention

Contact submissions are transmitted to a configured email-delivery provider, Resend. The website does not include a public database of enquiries. The site owner must determine its retention policy, access controls and applicable lawful basis before enabling delivery, and handle data-subject requests under relevant law.

Third parties and external resources

The site loads web fonts from Google Fonts and background imagery from Unsplash. These services may receive request-related technical information. Hosting and mail providers may process service logs. The site does not intentionally run advertising trackers.

Contact

For privacy enquiries, use the contact page. This notice should be reviewed by the site operator before production release.

An informed approach

Share context.
Protect sensitive information.

An investor-relations introduction often begins with commercial context. Sensitive documents, account information and confidential deal materials deserve an agreed secure channel, not a general form.

01 / GENERAL ENQUIRY

What to include

Your organisation, work contact information, relevant market or business theme and the reason for your enquiry.

02 / CONFIDENTIALITY

What to leave out

Non-public financial statements, personal identifiers, bank details and sensitive transaction documents.

03 / APPROPRIATE PROCESS

How to proceed

Agree on permissions and a suitable secure information-sharing route before exchanging protected documents.

Visitor information

Practical answers to
privacy questions.

Specific retention periods, lawful bases and organisational contact details require confirmation from the site owner.

Does the website use an advertising pixel?+

No advertising tracking pixel is intentionally included in the current website code.

Is a successful enquiry stored in a public database?+

The contact form code is designed to send an email and does not create an enquiry database. Hosting and delivery systems may still retain operational records.

Can I request information about my data?+

Please contact the site operator through an available professional channel. The responsible contact and retention policy should be finalised before the enquiry form is enabled.

Are external services used?+

Yes. Google Fonts and Unsplash are referenced for fonts and imagery, and the deployment and email services may process technical or submission-related information.
