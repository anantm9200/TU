Contact questions

Before you send
your enquiry.

Quick guidance on using the contact channel.

Can I send confidential investment material?+

Please avoid sending confidential documents or sensitive financial information through the general-purpose enquiry form. Agree on an appropriate channel before exchanging protected materials.

Will the form always deliver messages?+

No. Email delivery requires deployment configuration and testing. If delivery is not configured, the form displays an explicit error and does not claim success.

Will submitting create an investment relationship?+

No. An enquiry is only a request to communicate. It does not form an agreement, investment commitment or obligation to respond.
