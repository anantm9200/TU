Capital & risk

Make financial uncertainty discussable.

These examples are analytical prompts for internationally active agriculture businesses, not claims about IAG's financial condition.

01Seasonality and inventory+

How does the timing of planting, distribution and collection affect inventory and working-capital requirements?

Investor-relations response

Examine stock policy, cash-cycle assumptions and the reliability of collection information.

02Commodity and currency exposure+

How could changing procurement costs, market prices or currency conditions affect economics?

Investor-relations response

Clarify pricing policy, exposure ownership and appropriate sensitivities.

03Counterparties and logistics+

What happens if a counterparty, shipment or distribution channel performs differently from plan?

Investor-relations response

Consider counterparty review, contract terms and delivery contingencies.

04Governance and disclosure+

Which roles and approvals govern information given to external capital stakeholders?

Investor-relations response

Clarify controls, supporting evidence and the escalation path for unresolved matters.

Purpose, business priorities, markets, operating model and growth assumptions.

02

Financial context

03

Risk & governance
