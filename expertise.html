A practical information-handling sequence for investment-related conversations, adapted to the needs of the organisation and opportunity.

01Initial context+

Define the organisation, reason for engagement, geography and responsible contact.

02Business and capital narrative+

Describe strategic rationale, operating model, capital purpose and material assumptions.

03Evidence and documentation+

Coordinate the materials needed for a meaningful review, consistent with appropriate confidentiality.

04Open questions and ownership+

Assign information requests and identify questions that require specialist input.

05Next steps and reporting+

Keep decisions, dependencies and communication commitments visible to relevant parties.

Information architecture

What investors need is
an organised picture.

A clear hierarchy of information reduces friction while leaving room for scrutiny and independent diligence.

01

Strategic narrative

Purpose, business priorities, markets, operating model and growth assumptions.

02

Financial context

Historical information, forecasts where appropriate, and carefully qualified assumptions.

03

Risk & governance

Oversight, commercial risks, regulatory context and mitigation responsibilities.

04

Operating evidence

Capabilities, milestones, execution constraints and supporting documentation.

05

Transaction context

Proposed structure or objectives, if relevant and authorised for disclosure.

06

Communication discipline

Document versions, contacts, response timelines and an agreed reporting cadence.

Investor-relations design principles

Make every next step
easier to understand.

Good engagement is deliberate, not overcomplicated.

01

Be precise

Separate known facts from plans, assumptions and illustrative scenarios.

02

Be accountable

Keep information and decisions with the right owners.

03

Be consistent

Tell one coherent story across materials and meetings.

04

Be responsive

Close loops and communicate unresolved matters clearly.
