The questions behind
stronger relationships.

A few practical questions about the role of investor relations.

What does a global investor-relations function do?+

It coordinates the quality and consistency of dialogue between business leadership and current or potential investors, alongside relevant internal teams and advisers.

Does this website offer an investment product?+

No. This website is an executive profile and general information resource. It does not constitute an offer, solicitation, advice or a representation of availability.

How are prospective partnerships approached?+

Start with context, mutual objectives, relevant business information and a defined discussion process. Any commercial engagement is subject to appropriate review.

Can I request an introductory conversation?+

Yes. Use the contact page to introduce your organisation and explain the nature of the enquiry. Submission delivery depends on the website's email configuration.
