# TU leadership website design standard
- 3% content gutters, 1.5% header/footer gutters.
- Deep forest greens with warm golden-yellow gradients; restrained Manrope / DM Sans typography.
- Contextual finance-oriented photography and softly animated gradient paths that fade to the left; respect reduced motion.
- Every page has an immersive hero, meaningful prefooter and detailed footer.
- Mobile navigation, full keyboard focus, accessible contrast and graceful fallback without JavaScript.
- SEO, GEO and AI discovery: static semantic HTML, per-page titles/descriptions/canonicals, Open Graph, Person structured data, sitemap, robots and llms.txt.
- Avoid unverified biographical assertions, investment figures or endorsements.
